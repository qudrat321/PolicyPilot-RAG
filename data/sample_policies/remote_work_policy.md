# Global Remote Work and Equipment Policy

## 1. Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible to apply for remote work with departmental manager approval. Contractors and interns are not eligible for permanent remote status unless explicitly specified in their agreement.

## 2. Core Working Hours
Employees working remotely must be reachable and available for synchronous communication between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST), regardless of their geographic time zone.

## 3. Home Office Equipment Reimbursement
The organization provides a one-time home office equipment stipend of up to $500 USD for eligible remote employees. This stipend covers:
- Ergonomic desk chairs
- External computer monitors (maximum 27 inches)
- Keyboard and mouse accessories
- Surge protectors and monitor stands

Items such as personal desks, gaming accessories, home renovations, and coffee machines are non-reimbursable. All expense claims must be submitted within 45 calendar days of purchase with itemized receipts.

## 4. Internet Subsidy
Eligible full-time remote staff receive a monthly internet connectivity subsidy of $50 USD, processed automatically via payroll on the final business day of each month.
